Now probing the desktop menu bar itself (MG hover bridges over neighbour links).

CAUSE FOUND (desktop menu bar), 2.60.416 fdca57cc, Lane MG:
resources/css/kbb/kbb.css (HEAD line ~559-564) — the "hover bridges":
  .mbar .wrap > .navitem:is(.mg-l,.mg-a,.mg-s):hover::before/::after
  {position:absolute;z-index:69; inset-block-start:anchor(center); inset-block-end:anchor(end); ...}
While a mega item is hovered, two invisible triangles cover the LOWER HALF of the bar from
its link out to the ends of its panel — i.e. on top of the neighbouring menu links. Slide from
"Brands" to "Skincare" (or "Skincare" to "Sunscreens") a little below the text centre and:
  - the pointer is on DIV.navitem (the old item's bridge), not on the link;
  - the old panel stays open, the link under the pointer never gets hover -> no prefetch;
  - THE CLICK DOES NOTHING (no navigation) — the shopper has to click again.
Probe (tools/sx-neighbour.cjs, 1280px, y = 50/65/80% of link height):
  2.60.415: 15/15 clicks navigate.  2.60.416 and HEAD: 11/15 — every 65% and 80% case
  next to a mega item swallowed (hit DIV.navitem, clickNavigated=false).
Fix (CSS only): non-hovered menu links sit above the bridges (bridges z 68, idle links z 69,
panels stay 70), so a visible link always takes the pointer and the click.
