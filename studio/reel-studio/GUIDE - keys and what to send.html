KBB Studio guide: keys and what to send

Every item from the "what I need from you" list, step by step. Do them in any order; the studio works without all of them.

Keys are private. Paste a key only into ⚙ Configure inside KBB Studio, never into a chat, email or WhatsApp. The studio keeps keys in the private settings folder of KBB Studio Data in your home folder and shows only the last four characters.

1. Pexels photo key recommended · free

Free stock photos and videos (workers, cities, scenes) for the 📷 Photos tab.

  1. Go to pexels.com and click Join to make a free account (or log in).
  2. Open pexels.com/api and click Your API Key (or Get Started).
  3. Fill the short form: describe the project as "KBB Studio: photos for our own ads and reels", website kbeautybliss.com, tick the terms, and submit.
  4. Your key appears on the same page straight away. Click Copy.

Where to paste: KBB Studio → ⚙ Configure → Photo sources → Pexels → paste → Save → Test.

Free limit: 200 requests per hour and 20,000 per month, which is far more than daily use needs.

2. Unsplash photo key recommended · free

  1. Go to unsplash.com, click Join to make a free account, and confirm your email.
  2. Open unsplash.com/developers → Your apps → New Application.
  3. Tick all the guideline boxes → Accept terms.
  4. Application name: KBB Studio. Description: "Offline ad and reel editor for our own company posts". Click Create application.
  5. Scroll to Keys and copy the Access Key. Do not use the Secret key; the studio doesn't need it.

Where to paste: ⚙ Configure → Photo sources → Unsplash → paste the Access Key → Save → Test.

A new app starts in "Demo" mode: 50 requests per hour. That is enough to start. For more, press Apply for production on the app page later.

3. Claude key optional · paid per use

Everything works offline without it. With it, briefs are read more cleverly and translation gets a Best quality option. The studio asks before each session that uses it.

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign up (email and phone).
  2. Open Settings → Billing and add a payment card and a small credit (for example US$10). Optionally set a monthly spend limit in Limits.
  3. Open Settings → API Keys → Create Key, name it KBB Studio, and copy it. It starts with sk-ant- and is shown only once.

Where to paste: ⚙ Configure → Connect Claude → paste → Save → Test. The model stays on the default Claude Sonnet 5.5. Disconnect deletes the key from your Mac.

Cost: a brief or a page of captions usually costs a few US cents.

4. Planet Careers details needed for finished ads

Send these in the chat. They are not secret.

  • Phone and WhatsApp number to show on ads, plus email and website.
  • The call to action you want, e.g. "Book a free consultation", "WhatsApp us now".
  • A comment keyword for reels, e.g. comment VISA and we'll send details.
  • The countries you serve, and for each the real facts you are happy to show (intakes, job types, processing time). The studio never invents visa rules or salaries: they stay editable placeholders until you give them.

You can also type them yourself: ⚙ Configure → Contact & social (switch company at the top).

5. KBB handles and call to action needed for finished ads

  • Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and YouTube handles.
  • The call to action you like, e.g. "Shop now on kbeautybliss.com", "Order on WhatsApp".

Where: ⚙ Configure → Contact & social (company: KBB).

6. Your photos, videos and music makes ads look real

  • Planet Careers: photos of your team, happy clients (with their permission) and workers in uniform: nurse, driver, welder, chef and so on.
  • KBB: product photos and short videos of the shop, unboxing and textures.
  • Music you own or have bought (MP3/WAV). The built-in library is royalty-free already.

Where: drop them into the studio. In the editor, drag files onto the media bin, or put music in KBB Studio/library/music/user. Large files don't need to be sent to me.

7. Arabic and Urdu recordings finishes word-perfect sync

English captions already land within a few thousandths of a second. To make Arabic and Urdu as good, I need real voices.

  1. Record 3–4 short clips, 20–30 seconds each, on your phone or Mac. On a Mac use QuickTime Player → File → New Movie Recording.
  2. Talk normally, as in a real reel: two clips in Arabic and two in Urdu.
  3. Write down exactly what you said (the script) for each clip.
  4. Send the clips and scripts in the chat, or put them in KBB Studio/workspaces and tell me.

8. Arabic style one word answer

Tell me MSA (formal Modern Standard Arabic) or Gulf (Emirati and Khaleeji, friendlier) for captions and ad copy, or one for each company.

9. Banned words optional

Any words or claims ads must never use, e.g. "guaranteed visa", "100% approval", "cures acne". The studio will refuse them in every design and caption.

10. Your Mac details for speed tuning

  1. Click the Apple menu (top-left) → About This Mac.
  2. Send a screenshot (⇧ Shift ⌘ Cmd 4, then click the window after pressing Space). It shows the chip (M1, M2… or Intel), the memory and the macOS version.
  3. Tell me if you use Google Chrome (recommended) or Safari.

11. Apple Developer signing optional · US$99/year

Only needed so macOS stops showing "cannot be opened" warnings. Without it, the right-click → Open trick keeps working.

  1. Go to developer.apple.com/programs/enroll and sign in with your Apple ID (two-factor authentication must be on).
  2. Choose Individual, or Organization if you have a company D-U-N-S number, then pay US$99.
  3. Approval takes from a few hours to two days. Then tell me, and I'll send exact steps to make a signing certificate on your Mac. Certificates are never sent in chat.

Updating KBB Studio, or if something says "An older engine is running"

Close every Terminal window of KBB Studio, then double-click Start KBB Studio inside the new folder.

Updating is now just: replace the KBB Studio folder with the new one. There is nothing to copy. Your models, companies, projects, keys, music, saved photos and brand kits live in KBB Studio Data in your home folder (Finder → Go → Home), not in the KBB Studio folder, so a new version never asks for the models again. The first time the new version starts it moves anything of yours that is still in the old folder there by itself, and it finds the models of an older KBB Studio folder left in Downloads, Desktop, Documents or Applications. To back up everything, copy KBB Studio Data.
