# What's new, per version. tools/package-mac.sh puts the lines under "## <APP_VERSION>" into the package
# (update.json "notes"), and Configure → Updates shows them before he installs. One short line per change, his words,
# no jargon. A package is not built without a block for its version. Newest first.

## 1.4.7
- The "Do it for me" bar is one row now, with ‹ › arrows (or scroll it with the mouse wheel / trackpad). The preview gets the space back: about 46 px taller on every screen.

## 1.4.6
- 🏢 Office tour, in the Do it for me bar: a template for a walkthrough made of many clips (15+). One click makes the whole tour: a little room to move at every join, a transition on every join in a style (Signature, Calm, Energetic, Minimal, Cinematic), your room names, one colour across the clips and an end card. Then change it clip by clip: each join has its own transition, length and ▶ to watch it.
- Transitions now keep BOTH clips moving: the next clip plays its own footage leading into the cut instead of a frozen frame (when the clip has footage before its start point; Office tour's "room to move" makes sure it does).
- New transitions: Lens blur (out of focus, into focus) and Dip to white.
- Room names: small white text, no box, one per clip.
- Fixed: when a video frame could not be copied, the preview asked for it again and again until the browser ran out of memory.

## 1.4.5
- Edit the script, and it syncs by itself: in Captions → Script, change or paste a clip's words and press Enter — the studio listens again and puts every word on the voice straight away (no word-by-word timing). Undo works step by step.

## 1.4.4
- ⏱ Sync to voice, right in the Captions panel (beside Translate): after you edit the captions, one click listens again and puts every word back exactly on the voice. Only the timing moves — your words, styles and lines stay — and Undo brings the old timing back.
- After an edit the panel says "Captions edited — press ⏱ Sync to voice", and the note goes away once they are synced.

## 1.4.3
- Fixed: with Roman chosen, Auto captions on more clips wrote the new clips' captions in Hindi script (हिंदी) while the switch still said Roman. New words now always come in the script you chose, and captions already made this way are put back in Roman by themselves when you open the Captions panel (Undo brings them back).

## 1.4.2
- Fixed: Auto captions finished but wrote nothing on some Macs. When the Mac's graphics chip writes no words, the studio now writes the captions on the processor instead (slower the first time), and remembers to do that on this Mac.
- Captions can never sit on "Writing captions…" for ever: a stuck run stops with a message and ↻ Try again.
- When a clip gets no captions, the panel now says why for that clip, and 📋 Copy diagnostics copies a short report you can send.

## 1.4.1
- The 10 slim contact cards now wear Planet Careers' own colours in Planet Careers: navy with red icon circles, a red running light and a red neon outline (the red used to turn white). KBB's cards and the old designs are unchanged.

## 1.4.0
- 10 new slim contact cards that keep moving the whole time: Glass shimmer, Ticker strip, Running light, Ringing phone, Minimal underline, Gradient flow, Split chip, Typewriter number, Sparkle bar, Soft neon. About half the old height.
- Contact card controls: show or hide each part (icons, label, number, background, border, glow, sparkles, animation), a colour for each part, speed, intensity and corners.
- Text behind the host in country colours: every country and region in its own flag colours (stripes, solid or two-tone), changing with the carousel. Switch between White, Country colours and One colour, and change any country's colours by hand.
- Background music controls: volume up to 200%, mute, fade in and out, start the song at a set time, loop, and lower the music while someone speaks. Fixed: the volume slider did nothing on music added with "Add music".
- Double-click the music for the new Music Library: moods, search, click a song to hear it, "Preview with my video", then ✓ Use this song.
- 33 new original songs in viral-reel styles (bouncy trap, pop house, afro house, Amapiano, Arabic pop with darbuka, glossy R&B, luxury deep house, sale hype and more), yours to use in any ad.

## 1.3.3
- Text behind the host now has its own editor window beside the video, so the frame is never covered: tabs for Words, Look, Position and Cut-out.
- Every change shows on the video straight away; ✓ Apply keeps it (one undo step), Cancel puts it back exactly as it was.
- Open it by double-clicking the words on the video or timeline, or with ✎ Edit text behind… in the right panel, which is now short.
- Fixed: "Finding the person…" stayed on for ever; it now says when the person is found, or why not with ↻ Try again.
- Fixed: the words no longer run past the end of the video (they used to add black seconds to the export).
