# What's new, per version. tools/package-mac.sh puts the lines under "## <APP_VERSION>" into the package
# (update.json "notes"), and Configure → Updates shows them before he installs. One short line per change, his words,
# no jargon. A package is not built without a block for its version. Newest first.

## 1.3.3
- Text behind the host now has its own editor window beside the video, so the frame is never covered: tabs for Words, Look, Position and Cut-out.
- Every change shows on the video straight away; ✓ Apply keeps it (one undo step), Cancel puts it back exactly as it was.
- Open it by double-clicking the words on the video or timeline, or with ✎ Edit text behind… in the right panel, which is now short.
- Fixed: "Finding the person…" stayed on for ever; it now says when the person is found, or why not with ↻ Try again.
- Fixed: the words no longer run past the end of the video (they used to add black seconds to the export).

## 1.3.2
- Text behind the host: a big word like EUROPE behind the person in your video, the real background still there. Themes → Place elements → Text behind the host.
- Two lines if you want: JOBS IN on top (switch it off for the big word only), the country or a carousel of countries below.
- Country carousel: pick countries by continent or type your own; slide, flip, fade, typewriter or ticker, with small flags.
- One click: Themes → One click → Text behind me for every country I mention (follows your captions).
- Full controls: font, size, colours (UAE flag and country flag presets), outline, shadow, position (behind the head, centre, lower), animation in and out.

## 1.3.1
- 4 new National Day ads in Ad Studio: Glass & Dew and Skyline Dusk, line-up and scattered, in English and Arabic, with a UAE flag heading option.
- Every part of them is its own layer: logo, each line of text, the offer, the button, each product, the blobs, glass, dew, fireworks, skyline and floor.
- New heading style for any heading: Plain or UAE flag gradient (pick the heading → Heading style), with its four colours editable.
- Products can stand on a glossy floor with a reflection: pick a product → Shadow style → Soft + reflection.
- Duplicate a saved design in one click: My saved designs → Duplicate.
- Move a saved design to another company, or copy it there: My saved designs → Move / copy….
- Duplicate a video reel in one click: Home → Recent reels → ⧉.

## 1.3.0
- Install updates from inside the studio: Configure → Updates → Install update…, then pick the zip. No more replacing the folder.
- Roll back to the version you had before with one click, if anything looks wrong.
- Move a reel to another company: the ⇄ button next to ✕ in Recent reels. Its clips and look go with it.
- Copy a reel to another company from the same window.
- Phone videos now export the right way up (they came out on their side in a story export).
- Use any company's logo on any reel: Themes → One click → Logo of.
- Captions clip by clip: every clip is heard on its own (no more "no speech heard" on a normal voice), one caption bar per clip, and a box in Captions → Script to type a clip's own script.

## 1.2.1
- Captions clip by clip: each clip is heard on its own, with one caption bar per clip and a typed script per clip.
