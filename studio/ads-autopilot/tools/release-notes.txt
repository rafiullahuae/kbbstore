# What's new, per version. tools/package-mac.sh puts the lines under "## <VERSION>" into the package (update.json
# "notes"), and Settings → Updates shows them before he installs. One short line per change, his words, no jargon.
# A package is not built without a block for its version. Newest first.

## 0.3.0
- ✨ Create with Claude: one box (top bar, Overview, Creatives, or press C). Type what you want in Arabic or English; it picks the products, writes the words and makes every size plus a video. Change anything by typing ("shorter headline", "more pink"), then Run it, Save or Download.
- Use your Claude Max plan for creating: Settings → Connections → Claude desktop → Install connector, then make ads by chatting in the Claude app.
- When new ads are needed (tired ads, a free test slot, a season, a trending product) you get a Mac notification and a card with Start creating, which opens Claude with the brief ready.
- Creation is "With me in Claude (Max)" by default; creating automatically with API credits stays off unless you switch it on (Settings → Limits → Creation).
- One optional key for AI pictures and video (Google Gemini); the other providers moved to Settings → Advanced.
- Creatives → Made with Claude keeps every creation and which ones won.

## 0.2.0
- Your first ads go out right away: on the first run in Auto (with limits set) it launches a starter campaign, Arabic first, using about 70% of your limits; Practice shows exactly what it would launch.
- Approve or turn down one suggestion at a time (Approvals → Approve / Not now).
- Much better ad designs: a proper AI cut-out of each product (downloaded once), 10 elegant designs in Arabic and English for every size, bigger products, better motion videos.
- AI backgrounds and short AI videos (OpenAI, Gemini, FLUX, Ideogram, Veo, Runway, Kling, Luma): add a key in Settings → Connections → Generators; the cost is shown before every run and a monthly budget (default $25) is never passed. Your real product photo always stays on top.
- Your own videos can be uploaded to Meta from the Videos screen; the library shows your real ad pictures.
- Choose the ad wording rules in Settings → Limits (Strict / Balanced / Relaxed).

## 0.1.0
- First version: connects to Meta (with or without a verified business), Claude and the shop on open, reads every ad account you can see, and catches up on what happened while the Mac was closed.
- Runs each ad account on its own, inside the limits you set for it (and an optional limit for all accounts together). No limit set = nothing launches.
- Every ad type: pictures, videos, carousels, flexible ads, catalog ads, click-to-WhatsApp / Messenger / Instagram, retargeting and lookalike audiences.
- While the Mac is closed, Meta itself keeps the ads safe: the account spending limit and Meta's automated rules pause anything that spends without selling or goes over your daily limit.
- Learns from your whole history: up to 3 years of every account's results.
- A kill switch that stops everything the app made, in every account, at once.
