# What's new, per version. tools/package-mac.sh puts the lines under "## <VERSION>" into the package (update.json
# "notes"), and Settings → Updates shows them before he installs. One short line per change, his words, no jargon.
# A package is not built without a block for its version. Newest first.

## 0.6.6
- "Run a cycle" now says at once that it started (and the button shows "Running…" until it ends), then what it did: how many changes were made on Meta, what waits for your approval, the first launch, and any error. A full cycle can take a few minutes; before, nothing showed until it was over.
- Longer messages stay on screen long enough to read.

## 0.6.5
- Your limits are now for the autopilot's own ads only, as you asked. Your own campaigns — their budgets, their spend and their ads — are no longer counted: the daily limit, the monthly limit and Max active ads apply to the "KAA:" campaigns alone. This unblocks the first launch ("no room for a starter campaign … AED 1500 already committed" was your own campaigns' budgets).
- Meta's account spending limit now adds both: what is spent + what the autopilot may still spend this month + what your own campaigns' daily budgets need until month end. It moves with your budgets, so it never stops your own ads.
- New Meta rule "KAA: monthly limit" (with the existing "KAA: daily limit") holds the autopilot's own campaigns to your limits while the Mac is closed.
- Overview → Your limits shows the autopilot's own spend today and this month.

## 0.6.4
- Fixed: "Run it" only marked the ads approved and asked the planner to prefer them — nothing made them go live, and nothing showed it. Now Run it creates a job "Run your ads made with Claude" that puts exactly those ads live in your prospecting ad set at once (Auto or Practice), inside your limits. Plan → Upcoming jobs (and Recent jobs) shows it with every step; Campaigns shows the ads named "Made with Claude · …".
- If your Max active ads is full, Run it now says so at once ("no free ad slot: N active, your maximum is M — raise it to X") instead of quietly waiting. The autopilot never pauses a running ad to make room for new ones without results.
- Ads you pressed Run it on before this version (and that are not on Meta) get the same job by themselves at the next cycle.

## 0.6.3
- Create window: every idea shows its products. ✕ takes a product out, "+ Add or swap a product" puts one in — the words and the design stay, the photos and price follow (no Claude call).
- Create window: "↻ Redraw & save" on each idea and "↻ Redraw all & save" for the whole set: the SAME design drawn again in every size and the video, and saved to the library — including sizes the quality check used to hold back (they were the "missing" ones; now they are saved and you are told). "Regenerate" still means a NEW design from Claude.
- Approvals → Occasions: Regenerate with three choices — Ad copy (new words now, today's prices, same pictures), Creatives (new designs made by Claude on your Max plan; they replace the package's ads when saved) or Complete. Every regenerate puts the package back to waiting for your approval. "Goes live on" + Reschedule moves the date.
- Prices written in ad words (e.g. "AED 138" when the shop sells it at 108) are corrected automatically too, like the pictures.

## 0.6.2
- Prices, permanent fix: every saved picture and video now records the price printed on it. Whenever the app opens, and after it reads the shop again (a sale starting or ending), it compares that with the shop's price and redraws, by itself, any ad that is different. Ads running on Meta are replaced by the corrected version in the same ad set. Nothing else in the designs changes.
- Fixed: after "Correct prices" some ads still showed the old price. The Create window kept an old copy of the set (it now always shows the latest); the library kept showing the old picture until the app restarted (it now shows the new one at once); some pictures were missed (a headline edited after saving, or a design corrected earlier); and a few were skipped by the quality check ("product 39% of the frame") — a price correction is never skipped now.
- Sets (one product whose photo shows several items, like the Anua kit) are no longer repeated three times in the "Routine steps" design: each product photo is drawn once. Claude is told a set is one product. Saved ads with a repeated photo are redrawn automatically too.
- Creatives → Made with Claude → "Fix all ads" does the same at any time, and says what it changed.

## 0.6.1
- Prices: every ad now shows the price the shop charges today — the sale price when there is one (it showed the regular price). Claude is told the sale price too.
- Creatives → Made with Claude → "Correct prices on all ads": redraws every saved picture and video with the sale price. The designs stay exactly as they are (same photo, colours, words); only the price changes. Ads already running on Meta are replaced by the corrected version in the same ad set.

## 0.6.0
- Ads are made automatically with Claude Code on this Mac (your Max plan): whenever new ads are needed and the app is open, Claude Code makes them in the background — no Start, no Enter. Settings → Connections → Claude Code on this Mac shows Signed in ✓, an Automatic On/Off switch and a Test button. Cards show "Being made by Claude Code on this Mac…" and then "Made: N ads". It never launches ads by itself unless the account is in Auto with limits set.
- Approving an occasion now clears its reminders at once; every notice has a ✕.
- Settings → Accounts: Automate is a clear On/Off button.

## 0.5.2
- Fixed: Claude desktop made the ads but could not save them ("send session_id"). Saving now works whatever Claude calls the session, and with no session named it saves the ads it just made.

## 0.5.1
- Cloud creation card: if GitHub or the app refuses the key, the reason now stays on the card (it used to flash by and leave "Not connected" with no reason).

## 0.5.0
- More ads, planned every day: Strategy → Rules → Activity level (Steady / Active, the default / Aggressive). Active keeps 8–12 live ads, adds 4–6 new ones every Monday and Thursday, replaces losers the same day and gives winners new hooks within 48 hours. A 2nd campaign only when your budget can feed it (the screen says why).
- Overview → Today's plan (Arabic + English): yesterday's results from your real orders, what changed, today's jobs with times, and "Today's ads: N to create — Start creating".
- Overview → Watching now: every live and new ad checked every hour (click rate, cost per click, add-to-carts), spend pacing and problems; early stops only on strong evidence; rejected ads reworded and replaced.
- Approved occasion packages go live whole; if your Max active ads is too low, it says so with "Raise to N and go live".
- Cloud creation on your Max plan while you're away: Settings → Connections → Cloud creation (GitHub) — paste your GitHub key, Save & test. Jobs go to your private repo kbb-ad-exchange, a scheduled Claude task on your Max plan makes the ads every 2 hours, and the Mac pulls them in and re-checks them when it opens ("Made in the cloud on your Max plan").

## 0.4.6
- Occasions now come with 5 products picked for the season (winter: barrier and rich creams, lip care, masks; summer: sunscreen, oil control), ranked by your real sales in the last 90 days, new arrivals and trends. Each shows units sold, New/Trending and why it fits.
- ✕ removes a product from that occasion and the next-best one takes its place at once (it won't come back there); ⊘ never promotes it anywhere.
- Find products refreshes the list with today's sales and stock and fills the empty places, keeping what you pinned.
- Products per occasion: Strategy → Occasions (3–8, default 5). Season tags per product: Strategy → Products (your choice wins).

## 0.4.5
- Fixed: after an update, Settings → Connections showed empty boxes and "Not connected" until the app finished reconnecting, which looked like your keys had vanished. It now shows your saved keys (•••• last 4) with "Checking…" and turns green by itself when the test finishes. Your keys were not lost: don't paste them again.

## 0.4.4
- Choose the products yourself: in Approvals, Plan → Upcoming jobs and Approvals → Occasions, every ad shows its products. Change products opens a picker (search, photo, price, stock, new / trending badges); 📌 Pin puts a product first; ⊘ Don't promote this product again removes it everywhere (with Undo).
- If every product of a planned ad was avoided, it says so and waits for you to choose.

## 0.4.3
- Fixed: an update could sit on "Pause the autopilot" while a long sync or cycle was running. Updates now stop it safely after 10 seconds and carry on; the next cycle picks up where it stopped.
- Includes 0.4.1 (Pages and pixels found with a system-user key, Link by hand) and 0.4.2 (keys kept in a backup so updates can't empty them).

## 0.4.2
- Your keys can no longer disappear after an update: every key is now also kept in a backup in your "KBB Ads Autopilot Data" folder (readable only by your Mac user, never touched by updates). If the Mac's Keychain doesn't give a key back, the backup does, and the Keychain is repaired by itself. Paste your keys once more after this update; from then on they stay.

## 0.4.1
- Fixed: with a system-user key, Auto-link said "no business in the portfolio owns this ad account" and showed no Pages or pixels. The app now finds your business, Pages, Instagram and pixels through the ad account itself.
- Settings → Accounts → Link by hand: choose each ad account's Facebook Page, Instagram, pixel and catalog from a list, or paste the id; each link is checked with Meta when you save.

## 0.4.0
- Strategy (new, top of Think): how it decides with your real costs (VAT first, Tabby/Tamara 7%, cards 2.5%, cash 1.5%, Quiqup AED 15, your gift cost), your Playbook (your own instructions, Focus/Avoid products, prefer new & trending, audiences, budget split, offers: free gift, GLOW, pay in 4), ranked products with reasons, rules you can change, occasions and "Our playbook".
- Professional campaigns: one Advantage+ Sales campaign for new customers (past buyers left out) and Advantage+ catalog remarketing for people who viewed or added to cart; lookalikes once you have enough buyers; every setting shown with why. No WhatsApp ads.
- Plan → Upcoming jobs: Approve, Edit, Skip, Earlier/Later, see the brief Claude gets, or ask Claude to replan with your note.
- Occasions: National Day, White Friday, Ramadan, Eid and more — ads prepared in advance and waiting in Approvals → Occasions; nothing seasonal goes live without your approval.
- Benefit-first ads with a sales pitch; never a % or amount off, no gift tiers.
- Self-learning: it scores every decision against what really happened and improves its choices; a weekly "What I learned and changed" report.
- Approvals always says why it is empty, with a one-click fix; Settings → Accounts → Auto-link for Page, Instagram, pixel and catalog.
- Warms itself up when you open it or wake the Mac (Updating… / Up to date), and waits for the internet if it's offline.
- Keys: clear messages when macOS won't hand them over, never "not set"; settings are never overwritten after a read error; clearer Claude error messages.

## 0.3.1
- Settings → Updates: drop in an update or a small patch, review it, Install. It checks every file, restarts itself and checks its own health; if anything fails it goes back to your previous version by itself. Roll back is one button.
- Keys: if macOS doesn't let the app read its saved keys it now says so (click Always Allow, then Try again) instead of showing them as not set. A key pasted into the wrong box is refused.
- If the app is updated while the old engine is still running, a banner tells you to close the Terminal window and start again. Settings cards never disappear silently.

## 0.3.0
- ✨ Create with Claude: one box (top bar, Overview, Creatives, or press C). Type what you want in Arabic or English; it picks the products, writes the words and makes every size plus a video. Change anything by typing ("shorter headline", "more pink"), then Run it, Save or Download.
- Use your Claude Max plan for creating: Settings → Connections → Claude desktop → Install connector, then make ads by chatting in the Claude app.
- When new ads are needed (tired ads, a free test slot, a season, a trending product) you get a Mac notification and a card with Start creating, which opens Claude with the brief ready.
- Creation is "With me in Claude (Max)" by default; creating automatically with API credits stays off unless you switch it on (Settings → Limits → Creation).
- One optional key for AI pictures and video (Google Gemini); the other providers moved to Settings → Advanced.
- Creatives → Made with Claude keeps every creation and which ones won.

## 0.2.0
- Your first ads go out right away: on the first run in Auto (with limits set) it launches a starter campaign, Arabic first, using about 70% of your limits; Practice shows exactly what it would launch.
- Approve or turn down one suggestion at a time (Approvals → Approve / Not now).
- Much better ad designs: a proper AI cut-out of each product (downloaded once), 10 elegant designs in Arabic and English for every size, bigger products, better motion videos.
- AI backgrounds and short AI videos (OpenAI, Gemini, FLUX, Ideogram, Veo, Runway, Kling, Luma): add a key in Settings → Connections → Generators; the cost is shown before every run and a monthly budget (default $25) is never passed. Your real product photo always stays on top.
- Your own videos can be uploaded to Meta from the Videos screen; the library shows your real ad pictures.
- Choose the ad wording rules in Settings → Limits (Strict / Balanced / Relaxed).

## 0.1.0
- First version: connects to Meta (with or without a verified business), Claude and the shop on open, reads every ad account you can see, and catches up on what happened while the Mac was closed.
- Runs each ad account on its own, inside the limits you set for it (and an optional limit for all accounts together). No limit set = nothing launches.
- Every ad type: pictures, videos, carousels, flexible ads, catalog ads, click-to-WhatsApp / Messenger / Instagram, retargeting and lookalike audiences.
- While the Mac is closed, Meta itself keeps the ads safe: the account spending limit and Meta's automated rules pause anything that spends without selling or goes over your daily limit.
- Learns from your whole history: up to 3 years of every account's results.
- A kill switch that stops everything the app made, in every account, at once.
