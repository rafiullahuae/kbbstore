Lane S8, 2026-09-26. Closes `SEO-ROUND-5-VERIFICATION.md` §7 item 6 — *"Approve
six article topics"* — which could not be closed because there were no six topics
to approve. `SEO-BUILD-PLAN.md` item 6 names a list and says of it, in its own

**This file is the decision put in front of you.** Six rows, each one a yes or a
no. Nothing here is written yet and nothing should be written until you have
said which.

---

## How to use this

Against each topic, write **YES**, **NO**, or **LATER**. That is the whole ask.
You do not need to research anything and you do not need to write anything.

Three things worth knowing before you start.

**1. The topics are not a wish list; they are the six holes in what you already
have.** This shop has five real articles, and three of them are about sensitive
skin, heartleaf and barrier repair (`SEO-CONCERN-MAPPING.md` §6). So
`/concern/sensitivity/` can launch with three real outbound links, and the other
seven concern pages can launch with **none**. Every topic below fills one of
those gaps rather than adding a fourth article about cica.

**2. No traffic claim is made anywhere in this file, for any term, in either
language.** There is no search-volume figure in this corpus and nobody in this
project has Search Console access yet. "Search intent" below describes the *shape*
of the query — what somebody who typed it is trying to do — which is a judgement
about language, not a measurement. If you want the numbers before you decide, the
answer is item 7 on the outstanding list: turn Search Console on.

**3. Each article is a commission with a fee, and the Arabic is a second one.**
`SEO-BUILD-PLAN.md` item 2's conclusion stands: machine-translated Arabic is worse
than no Arabic, and Gulf product search skews to dialect over Modern Standard
Arabic. Six articles in English is six commissions. Six in both is twelve. You
can say yes to the English and defer the Arabic, and that is a normal answer.

## What is NOT on this list, and why

Three topics that were in the original proposal and are not here.

**Cica / centella / heartleaf for redness.** `SEO-BUILD-PLAN.md` item 6 ranks
this first. **It already exists** — *Heartleaf extract: transforming K-beauty
skincare* — and so do two more articles serving the same concern. Commissioning a
fourth piece about calming ingredients would be the only genuinely wasted
commission available. `/concern/sensitivity/` is the one concern page that needs
no new writing at all; it needs its three existing articles linked to it, which
is an editing job on pages you already own.

**Snail mucin.** An ingredient rather than a concern, and it does not map onto any
of the eight concern pages — it would be an article with nowhere to send a reader.
Worth doing one day as part of an ingredient series; not one of the first six,
because the first six exist to give the concern pages something to link to.

**Dullness and glow.** The eighth concern, and the one left without an article on
purpose. Its shelf overlaps heavily with hydration (topic 4) and dark spots
(topic 2), so a piece of its own would mostly repeat them, and six is the number
you were asked to approve. If you want a seventh, this is the one to add.

## And two things that are not article commissions but do the same job

Both are editing work on pages that already exist, and both are cheaper than any
row above.

1. **Link the three existing sensitive-skin articles to `/concern/sensitivity/`,
   and link that page back to them.** Three real bidirectional links, no writing.
   `SEO-CONCERN-MAPPING.md` §6 names the exact slugs and the anchor text to use.
2. **Give the articles that have no excerpt one.** An article with no excerpt
   publishes no meta description at all — Google writes the line under the title
   itself. `SEO-ROUND-5-VERIFICATION.md` §9 item 4 records it, the SEO Audit
   screen already lists which articles are affected, and the fix is a sentence per
   article at Content → Blog Posts.
