Checkout email hint, on a phone

While the shopper types, nothing appears. Only when they leave the email field (tap the next field or Done) does the shop check the address. If it is a clear slip such as yahoo.co, one small line offers the fix. One tap accepts it; ignoring it changes nothing and never blocks the order. A correct address never shows anything. Three styles, each on an iPhone (390), a typical Android (360) and a small phone (320).

The rules behind it. Only the part after @ is checked. It suggests only a known provider (gmail.com, yahoo.com, hotmail.com, outlook.com, icloud.com and the like), and real addresses like yahoo.co.uk, hotmail.fr or company domains never get a hint. It gives one suggestion or none, and it never edits the field by itself. It runs in the shopper’s browser: no request, no added server load. On/off: Emails → Settings.
