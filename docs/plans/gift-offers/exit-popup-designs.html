How it will work

The gift follows the bag value

You set cart-value ranges, the same way as the free gift box, and choose one full-size product for each. The popup shows the gift for the range the shopper's bag is in at the moment she tries to leave. Below the first range, no popup. Switch the bag value under the phone to see it change.

If she claims and then changes her bag: the gift stays while the bag is still in its range. If the bag drops below that range, the gift leaves the bag, and the row says why. If the bag grows into a higher range, she keeps the gift she claimed. She can't claim a second one.

Who sees it

  • Setting "Who sees it": everyone with a product in her bag, or only shoppers who reached checkout and did something there (typed a detail, picked delivery or payment)
  • Checkout activity is noticed in the page itself: no extra request, nothing sent while she types
  • Once per bag; after "Maybe later", not again for 24 hours
  • Only while the gift product is in stock
  • Only when the bag is inside one of your ranges
  • Leaving checkout counts as leaving (closing it, or going back to the shop), but never once she has pressed Place order
  • Never on the account pages or the order-received page

When she taps Claim

  • The product goes into her bag as quantity 1 at AED 0
  • The cart page opens, with the thin timer row under the gift box
  • The same row shows in the cart panel and the checkout summary
  • The timer is kept by the shop's server, so a reload, a new tab or a wrong phone clock doesn't reset it

When the 20 minutes run out

  • The gift leaves the bag by itself, and the row says so for a moment
  • A checkout started in time is honoured if she places the order within the timer
  • The server checks the timer again when the order is placed, so the gift can't be kept by any trick
  • The minutes are a setting: 20 by default

Fair and safe

  • One claim per bag, and one per customer or phone in 24 hours
  • The gift doesn't count towards the free gift box ranges or coupon minimums
  • It is on the order as a gift line, like the gift box items, so stock goes down correctly
  • "Maybe later" is a plain, polite choice, never "No, I hate free things"

Light and fast

  • About 3 KB of script, loaded only when the bag has items
  • Built from what the page already has: no request until she taps Claim
  • Nothing is added to the head of any page; the speed rules stay untouched
  • The product photo uses the shop's small image sizes

In the admin

Growth & Marketing → Exit offer: on/off, the cart-value ranges with one full-size product each, the minutes (20), the design and the words in English and Arabic.

Counts in Cart Tracking: shown, claimed, and became an order. They also go to the owner app, with a "Claimed exit gift" tag on the cart.
