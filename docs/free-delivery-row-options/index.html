Free delivery row for the cart panel

Shows only when the bag total reaches the free-delivery amount, above the coupon row and Subtotal. Every option stays on one line at the phone panel width (278px) and the laptop panel width (340px): the text sizes itself to the row, like the coupon row. Each opens with a celebration icon and ends with a green tick. Pick a letter, or mix a design with another option's wording.

Placement. The panel already says “You’ve unlocked free delivery!” in the progress bar at the top. This row repeats the good news at the bottom, beside Checkout, where the shopper is about to decide. It never shows below the free-delivery amount, and it uses the same amount as the bar, so the two can never disagree. Each option comes with Arabic wording for /ar.
