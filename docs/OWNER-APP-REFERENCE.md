## What we keep, what we beat
Keep the information architecture (4 bottom tabs, grouped orders, status pills,
detail sections, row-to-editor pattern). Beat it: bulk selection on orders,
low-stock view, faster search across orders / products / customers, live
refresh with the blurred "Refreshing the app" overlay, push notifications,
PIN unlock, our own branding (not WooCommerce purple unless chosen).
