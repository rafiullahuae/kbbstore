Why the Ingredients box looks empty on almost every product. On the product page, the ingredients tab comes from one of two places:

1. The product's own Ingredients box (Catalog → Products → search the name → Ingredients box). Only 10 products have it filled in; their tab is called “Ingredients”.

2. A separate per-product tab called “Major Ingredients”, brought over from the old WordPress shop. 345 products show their ingredients this way, so the list is on the shop even though the Ingredients box in the editor is empty. It is edited in Catalog → Product tabs (pick the product).

The 399 products below have neither, so the shop shows no ingredients tab at all. To fix one: Catalog → Products → search the name → paste the full list into the Ingredients box → Save. The tab appears by itself once the box has text.

Most affected brands (products missing): Medicube 99, Anua 20, Arencia 20, VT Cosmetics 17, Celimax 14, EQQUALBERRY 12, APRILSKIN 11, Dr.Melaxin 11. 13 of the missing products are devices or tools that have no ingredients; they are marked and can be skipped.
