Six new mobile menus for K-Beauty Bliss

Every phone is live. Tap the four-tile icon at the top left to open it, tap a section to go inside, tap the dark area or swipe the panel to the left to close. Each one uses the shop's real menu.

The menu items come straight from the shop's own menu, the same 12 items and sub-items a phone sees today (Brands with 17 trending brands, Skincare with 10 categories, then Sunscreens through Blog). "Brands A–Z" uses the full 65-brand list kbeautybliss.com published. The Arabic names of the menu items are drafts written for this preview; the menu, account and search wording already exists in Arabic on the shop.

Recommendation: C · Picture Tiles

The shop is visual and so is she: tiles let her find Sunscreens or Lip Care by picture, the targets are the biggest of the six (108px tiles, 64px offer banners), and Super Sale and Under 54 AED get banners instead of a row lost in a list. It keeps the search and the quick chips from A, so nothing is lost for customers who type. It costs about the same to run as A.

What all six share

  • Opens from the left (the right in Arabic) with a 300ms slide that moves the panel only, never the page.
  • Tap the dark area, swipe toward the edge, press Esc or the × to close. Swipe the other way on a sub-page to go back.
  • Nothing is fetched when it opens: the menu is already in the page, the icons are drawn in code, search runs on the phone.
  • No new library, and no script that measures the page; sizes come from CSS.
  • Account, Wishlist, WhatsApp help and English / العربية at the bottom; every row at least 48px tall.

What is real here, and what is not

  • Real: the 12 menu items, their order and links, the 17 trending brands, the 10 Skincare categories, the 65-brand A–Z list, the WhatsApp number, and the Arabic wording for search, account, wishlist and close.
  • Drafts: the Arabic names of the menu items, and the “Popular right now” searches in E.
  • Links do not leave this page; a tap shows where it would go.
