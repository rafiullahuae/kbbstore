Four ways to offer shoppers the app, drawn over the real homepage at iPhone, Android and iPad sizes. Tap the buttons below the device to walk each option through every moment: browsing, the offer, the install steps, opened from the Home Screen, and after "Not now".

Checked, not assumed

Full screen ends on every page change.Measured in Chromium on this shop: full screen true on the homepage, false one tap later on /shop/. The owner app keeps full screen because it never leaves its page; the shop loads a new page per tap. So on the shop the "expand" icon cannot be a full-screen switch. It opens the install instead, and the installed app is what stays full screen.
WhatsApp owns the bottom-right corner.Measured at 390 and 820 wide: 60×60, 48px above the bottom, 44px in from the right. A uses the same 72px lift the sticky Add to Bag bar already gives it; B sits on the opposite side, centred on the same line, and swaps sides with it.
The bottom of a product page is taken.The sticky Add to Bag bar and the docked cart bar live there. A and B step aside on those pages; C and D are never near them.
iPhone keeps the Home Screen app's storage apart from Safari.That is Apple's design: a shopper who is signed in, or has a bag, in Safari opens the new app signed out with an empty bag. Asking right after an order (C) is when this costs them nothing. Phase 2 confirms it on a real iPhone.
The notch only matters inside the app.The shop's viewport tag does not ask for the full screen edge to edge, so in Safari nothing moves. In the installed app the plan turns edge-to-edge on and pads the header, bottom bar, WhatsApp and drawers by the safe-area insets, in rules that apply only when opened from the Home Screen.
The bottom tab bar is off on this install.Store & content → Floating bottom menu (mobile) is off by default. If you switch it on, A and B sit above it.
