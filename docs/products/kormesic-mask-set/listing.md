# KORMESIC 9-piece sheet mask set: product listing draft

Prepared 24 October 2026 from the owner's Amazon screenshot and the pack texts, checked against web search.

## READ FIRST: origin and claims

1. **KORMESIC does not appear to be a Korean brand.** The trademark records found are a US filing (Dec 2022, serial 97729892) and a Canadian one (2262619). Both name **Qingyuan Oubo Cosmetics Co., Ltd., Qingyuan, Guangdong, China**, renamed in 2025 to Qingyuan Hanxiang Biotechnology Co., Ltd. The brand's own wholesale site (kormesicglobal.com) lists a KORMESIC mask with **origin: China**. kormesic.com describes the company as an OEM/ODM private-label mask manufacturer. Indonesia: PT Sing Foo Cosmetic, Jakarta, holds BPOM registrations for the brand. The packs carry Korean text, but nothing found says they are made in Korea.
   **So the listing below does NOT say "Korean", "made in Korea" or "K-beauty brand".** Before publishing, check the "Made in" line on the physical box. If it says Korea, the wording can be changed.
2. **No INCI list was found** for these three 25 ml masks. The ingredients below are the ones named on the pack fronts. Add the full list from the back of the sachets before publishing.
3. **"99% 감초추출물" (99% licorice extract)** is printed on both the green and the pink pack. It is unverified and hard to believe for a sheet-mask essence, so the listing names licorice extract without the 99%.
4. **"Whitening" (화이트닝/미백) is written as "brightening".** Whitening and anti-melasma claims (기미 감소) are treated as medicinal claims in UAE cosmetic rules, and they are poor wording for the shop anyway.
5. No certifications (cruelty-free, dermatologist-tested, vegan, GMP for this product) could be verified, so none are claimed.

## English

**Product name:** KORMESIC - Sheet Mask Set (9 pcs) - Centella, Niacinamide & Hyaluronic Acid

**Brand:** KORMESIC

**Short description:**
Nine sheet masks for three skin moods: 3 Centella Asiatica masks to soothe, 3 Niacinamide masks to brighten and 3 Hyaluronic Acid masks for deep hydration. Each sheet holds 25 ml of essence. Use whichever one your skin needs that day.

**Long description:**

A simple at-home mask routine in one box. The set has three masks of each type, so you can pick the one your skin needs: calming after a long day, brightening before an event, or a big drink of moisture after sun and air-conditioning.

**Green: Centella Asiatica Soothing Mask (x3)**
- Helps support the skin barrier
- Boosts moisture
- Helps calm the look of redness and puffiness
- Key ingredients on the pack: Centella Asiatica extract, licorice root extract

**Pink: Niacinamide Brightening Mask (x3)**
- Helps even out skin tone
- Brightens a dull complexion
- Helps fade the look of dark spots over regular use
- Key ingredients on the pack: Niacinamide, licorice root extract

**Blue: Hyaluronic Acid Hydrating Mask (x3)**
- Deep hydration for dry or tight-feeling skin
- Leaves skin soft, plump and dewy
- Key ingredient on the pack: Hyaluronic Acid

**Suits:** all skin types. Centella for sensitive, stressed or red-prone skin; Niacinamide for dull or uneven tone; Hyaluronic Acid for dry and dehydrated skin. Good for skin dried out by heat and air-conditioning.

**How to use:**
1. Cleanse your face and apply toner.
2. Unfold the sheet and place it on your face, lining it up with your eyes and mouth.
3. Leave it on for 15–20 minutes.
4. Remove the sheet and gently pat the remaining essence into your skin. No need to rinse.
5. Follow with your moisturiser. Use 2–3 times a week, or whenever your skin needs it.

**Contents:** 9 sheet masks (3 Centella Asiatica, 3 Niacinamide, 3 Hyaluronic Acid), 25 ml / 0.85 fl.oz each.

**Storage & caution:** Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. For a cooling effect, chill a mask in the fridge before use. For external use only. Avoid the eye area. Patch-test before first use. Stop using if irritation occurs. Do not reuse a sheet. Keep out of reach of children.
