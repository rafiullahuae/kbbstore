2 Mbit/s down and up, 40 ms RTT, gzip on (tools/perf-router.php), 1280x860, owner account.

  Before the change, the 92 NAV rows appeared at buildNav() (~1.5 s) and the 93rd,
  #KBeautyBliss Spotted (in neither NAV nor LATE_NAV), only at DOMContentLoaded (~6 s).
  On the first build of this lane, a throttled hard refresh painted 57 of 93 rows for 217 ms
  because the line had delivered half of the <nav>. The <nav> now carries data-wait
  (visibility:hidden) until the script printed after </nav> runs, so it paints whole.

  Rejected after measuring: serving the files to a browser that does not have them. Cold
  went to sidebar 1.1-1.5 s and dashboard 4.4 s, because ninety parallel files share the
  line. Hence the cookie gate (App\Support\AdminConsoleAssets::serve).
