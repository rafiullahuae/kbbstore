Shipping & Delivery
At K-Beauty Bliss, we are committed to delivering your orders swiftly and efficiently, both locally and internationally. Below, you’ll find all the essential details regarding our delivery services:
Local Delivery:
For orders within UAE, we offer delivery to your doorstep. Once your order is placed, we offer delivery within only 1-3 days all over UAE.
For any inquiries or assistance regarding local delivery, feel free to reach out to our dedicated customer support team.
International Delivery:
For our valued customers worldwide, we extend our delivery services to various international destinations.
After placing your order, please allow 2-5 business days for processing before shipment. International delivery times may vary depending on your country’s customs regulations and local postal services. Typically, delivery can be expected within 7-14 business days from the date of dispatch.
To ensure a smooth delivery experience, we collaborate with reputable international carriers who prioritize the safety and timely delivery of your packages. Once your order is shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number, enabling you to track your package every step of the way.
Please note that additional customs duties, taxes, or import fees may apply depending on your country’s regulations. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the total purchase price or shipping cost.
Customs / Import

* For international orders, please check your country’s and city’s import policy before placing the order. In case of any custom or import restrictions, K-Beauty Bliss will not be responsible once the order has been shipped.
* If customers refused to pay the customs tax, that it is a part of customers responsibility, refund is NOT possible in this case.
* Please note that after it is confirmed that customers refuse to pay customs tax until the determined period decided by the shipping company, the whole package will be disposed of, and even when customers change their mind and want to pay it, as it is already too late, refund or redelivery is not possible.
* In case of incorrect address or contact information on customers’ order, we will send an email requesting the correct information. If customers fail to provide the required information within the determined period decided by the shipping company, the whole package will be returned or disposed of.
* Please be aware that refund or redelivery is NOT possible in this case.

For any inquiries or assistance regarding international delivery, please don’t hesitate to contact our customer support team. We’re here to assist you throughout the shipping process and ensure your complete satisfaction with your K-Beauty Bliss purchase.
Thank you for choosing K-Beauty Bliss . We look forward to delivering exceptional products right to your doorstep, wherever you may be!
