What happens when you pick one

Reply with a letter (or a mix, such as “B, with A as its dark mode”). The chosen direction is applied to the real owner app, which another lane is building now. Nothing on this page is decoration: every screen and control shown — PIN unlock, the refresh overlay, bulk status changes, the editors, notifications, install to home screen — is a working part of that app, wired to the shop’s live orders, stock and customers.

All figures here are sample data shaped like the shop’s: real product names and prices, invented customers.
