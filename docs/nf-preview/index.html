Your 404 page — four options

Every page that does not exist on the shop will show one of these instead of the plain “404 | Not Found”. Each one sits inside your real header, menu, cart and footer (shown here as grey placeholders). Each frame is live: hover the buttons, and the motion is the real motion. Pick a letter.

Recommended: B — the slipping sheet mask. It is the only one that says both of your lines word for word (“Aah, you shouldn’t be here… but you look gorgeous”), its face is the “broken face” you described without being sad or scary, and it is the most K-beauty of the four (sheet mask + bow headband). It also stays light: the whole illustration is about 4.6 KB of inline SVG, and on a 390px phone the headline and the “Take me home” button are on the first screen. Runner-up: A, if you want a darker, more luxurious look.

AMidnight vanity — the cracked compact

Dark plum “midnight vanity” hero, rose-gold compact; the mirror is split by a crack and the lower shard of her surprised face sits a few pixels off. 404 is pressed into the powder. Motion: compact floats, sparkles twinkle, the shard trembles.

Blush split layout. A cute face in a K-beauty bow headband; her sheet mask has slid down so its eye holes sit on her cheeks and her wobbly “broken” smile peeks through one of them. One wink, a sweat drop, an “aah!” bubble. Motion: mask sways, a drop of essence drips, bubble pops.

CLipstick smudge — the 404 written in lipstick

Full-bleed peach→pink→lilac gradient. A fallen lipstick has written a giant “404” — it draws itself on load (once, 2s) — with the cap rolled away and a serum spilled beside it.

DThe shelf with one product missing

Cream page, white copy card beside a pastel shelf under an arch: toner, cream jar, tube, pump, a vase of flowers — and one dashed, empty space with a swinging “404” tag. Motion: gap pulses, tag swings, flowers sway, a petal falls.

Same on all four
  • A “Take me home” button, a search box (it searches the shop exactly like the header search), and four links: Shop all, Super Sale, Best sellers, WhatsApp us.
  • “Trending now” shows four of your real best sellers on the live page (the cards here are samples). Cost: one database query, cached for an hour, so almost every 404 costs no query at all. It can be switched off.
  • The page answers with a real 404 status and tells Google not to index it, so a broken link never becomes a thin “page” in search results.
  • All motion stops for visitors whose phone asks for reduced motion. No new JavaScript, no images to download — the drawing is part of the page.
